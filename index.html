Suggested 60-day rhythm

DAYS 1–30

Build coverage

Study the Preliminary topics systematically. After each topic, solve topic-wise MCQs and record weak areas for revision.

DAYS 31–42

Strengthen and practise

Finish remaining topics, increase mixed MCQ practice, revisit weak areas and begin regular full-length mocks.

DAYS 43–52

Mock-test phase

Attempt full mocks under timed conditions. Analyse mistakes and revise the topics behind incorrect answers.

DAYS 53–60

Final revision

Revise short notes, repeated mistakes, formulas and facts. Keep practice steady and avoid leaving weak areas unattended.
